My Story

Hi, I'm Mark — your Performance Marketing and CRM Automation Specialist

My career started over 11 years ago when I realized that the gap between a business running ads and a business running a real revenue system was enormous. Ads without automation leak leads. Automation without the right traffic is wasted infrastructure. I built my skills across both so the clients I work with get the complete picture.

I have had the privilege of managing campaigns across healthcare, ecommerce, SaaS, fitness, professional services, financial services, travel, and home improvement. Each industry taught me something different about buyer behavior, compliance constraints, and what actually moves someone from interested to converted.

When I am not in Ads Manager or building out a workflow in GoHighLevel, I am probably testing a new funnel concept or analyzing why a creative that should work is not working.
